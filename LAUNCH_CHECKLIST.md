# Launch checklist

- [ ] Confirm wax, fragrance, wick, container compatibility and safety tests
- [ ] Add authentic finished-product photos, labels, prices, stock, and availability
- [ ] Complete seller identity, privacy, cookies, terms, returns, delivery and complaint details
- [ ] Configure shipping, payment recipient data, private orders storage, Resend and admin authentication
- [ ] Test checkout, legal text, accessibility and mobile layout on the Vercel preview
- [ ] Obtain owner approval before enabling checkout or merging to `main`
